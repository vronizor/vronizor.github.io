I am an applied microeconomist working as a data analyst at [Proton](https://proton.me) in the VPN business unit.

I was previously a Postdoctoral Researcher at the [Sustainable Long-Distance Mobility Chair](https://www.parisschoolofeconomics.eu/en/pse-partnership-programme/chairs/chair-sustainable-long-distance-mobility/) at the Paris School of Economics. Before that, I was a PhD candidate at Trinity College Dublin, where I was an [Irish Research Council](https://research.ie/) Scholar and affiliated to the [Trinity Impact Evaluation](https://www.tcd.ie/time/) research centre.

You can learn more about my research on [this page](docs/research/research.html). I occasionally [blog](docs/blog.html) about coding and data analysis. If you want to get in touch, feel free to [email me](mailto:vthorne[at]pm[dot]me), or book a slot during my [office hours](https://app.reclaim.ai/m/vinceth/office-hours).
